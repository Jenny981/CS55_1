# CS55 Evidence System Wiki

CS55 links sampled Final frames to Storyboard and Animatic evidence, records human corrections, and reports visual support, ordering, declared correspondence, provenance and timestamps. It supports evidence review; it does not establish authorship, AI origin or authenticity.
